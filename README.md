## Currently building

### [PvZ-Portable](https://github.com/mr-jones123/PvZ-Portable) · `C++` `RL`
Plants vs. Zombies, modded to the difficulty of the **Total Death** mod (roughly 8x zombie spawn rate, widely considered the hardest PvZ difficulty mod). Two agents fight over it:

| Agent | Policy | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **DDQN + MLP** | off-policy, value-based | Which trains faster? |
| **PPO + MLP** | on-policy, actor-critic | Which actually beats the game? |

### [Soup-MLX](https://github.com/mr-jones123/Soup-MLX) · `Python` `MLX`
Porting **Soup** to Apple MLX. Fine-tune LLMs from a single YAML file. Layer streaming lets an 8B model train on a 4 GB laptop GPU.

### [zenin](https://github.com/mr-jones123/zenin) · `Python` `Agents`
Growing into a full **pi benchmark** and a **self-evolving tool** that writes its own skills and extensions as it works.
